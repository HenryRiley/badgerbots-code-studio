These notes preserve requirements previously extracted from two BadgerBots-owned curriculum slide decks. The original attached PDFs were not available in the current filesystem, so detailed transcription must wait until they are copied to `curriculum/source-material/` and verified.

Confirmed planning observations:

- Younger sequence: Sheep City, High Noon Saloon, Super Powered, Tag, Lucky Blocks.
- Older sequence: Vanilla+, Spartan School, Wizarding 101, Zombie Tag, Lucky Blocks.
- Projects use Player, Game, and entity code areas; a searchable library; console errors; event methods; custom methods; fields/variables; commands; timers; conditionals; entities; inventories; gamemodes; explosions; effects; cooldowns; recurring logic; and operations targeted at other objects/entities.
- Sheep City centers on projectile/explosion behavior, movement/material detection and bounce, plus a configured sheep entity and drops.
- Vanilla+ was described as roughly 11 coding steps and includes setup, fields, custom methods, recurring friend tracking, switches, and commands.
- Existing course filtering sometimes hides needed blocks or fails progress recognition; BadgerBots requires the full library plus tolerant benchmarks.
